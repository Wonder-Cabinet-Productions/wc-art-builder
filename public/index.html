This tool generates art for new Wonder Cabinet episodes — the guest image goes on the right in the larger pane, and the left two images should tie into the conversation in interesting ways. From the same three images it also makes the Social Sharing Graphic, a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail, and quote cards for each pull quote.
